Amigurumi Golden Retriever puppy crochet pattern

Bring home a pair of forever-puppies with this free Golden Retriever Puppy Crochet Pattern! Designed with beginners and intermediate crocheters in mind, these little amigurumi retrievers are the perfect quick-win project. Whether you're making a gift for a dog lover or adding to your own plushie collection, these pups are sure to melt hearts.

To crochet this Golden Retriever puppy you will need:
* ﻿﻿Bulky (Weight 5) plush chenille yarn (120 m / 100 g) Himalaya Dolphin Baby
* ﻿﻿4.0 mm crochet hook
* ﻿﻿12mm safety eyes
* ﻿﻿16x12 mm safety nose
* ﻿﻿Fiberfill
* ﻿﻿Black embroidery floss
* ﻿﻿Cotton thread for making indents
* ﻿﻿Tapestry needle and scissors
Your finished amigurumi puppy will be approximately 18 cm (7") tall if using the recommended materials.

Skill level: intermediete

Abbreviations:
st = stitch
ch = chain
sc = single crochet
inc = increase (2 sc in one st
dec = decrease (2 sc together)
sl st = slip stitch
hdc = half double crochet
(..) = repeat the instructions in brackets the given number of times
I•] = total number of stitches in a round/row
Work in continuous rounds (spirals) unless otherwise specified. Mark the first stitch of each round with a stitch marker or a scrap of yarn.

Head:
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring [6]
Rnd 2: (inc) repeat 6 times [12]
Rnd 3: (sc in next st, inc) repeat 6 times [18]
Rnd 4-5: sc in all 18 st [18]
Rnd 6: sc in next 3 st, (hdc inc) repeat 12 times, sc in next 3 st [30]
Rnd 7: sc in next 4 st, inc, (hdc in next 4 st, hdc inc) repeat 4 times, sc in next 4 st, inc [36]
Rnd 8-12 (5 rounds): sc in all 36 st [36]

Safety eye placement: Insert eyes in Rnd 6. Place the first eye after the 3rd increase and the second eye after the 8th increase, leaving 5 increases between them.
Safety nose placement: Center the nose between Rnd 2 and Rnd 3, ensuring it is vertically aligned with the midpoint of the eyes.
Stuff firmly with fibertill.
Rnd 13: (sc in next 4 st, dec) repeat 6 times [30] Rnd 14: (sc in next 3 st, dec) repeat 6 times [24] Rnd 15: (sc in next 2 st, dec) repeat 6 times [18]
Add more stuffing
Rnd 16: (sc in next st, dec) repeat 6 times [12.
Rnd 17: (dec) repeat 6 times 16]
Fasten off. Weave the yarn tail through the front loops of the remaining 6 stitches with a hook or yarn needle, then pull to close the hole.

Arms (make 2):
Crochet 4 ch. Continue working around the foundation chain, beginning in 2nd ch from the hook.
Rnd 1: sc in next 2 ch, 3 sc in last ch, turn, sc in next ch, inc [8]
Rnd 2: inc, sc in next st, (inc) x 3 times, sc in next st, (inc) x 2 times [14]
Rnd 3: sc in all 14 st [14]
Rnd 4: (sc in next 5 st, dec) repeat 2 times [12]
Rnd 5: sc in next 2 st, dec, sc in next 8 st [11] Rnd 6: (dec) x 3 times, sc in next 5 st [8]
Rnd 7-12 (6 rounds): sc in all 8 st [8]
Lightly stuff the hand only. Leave the rest of the arm unstuffed so it hangs naturally against the body. Sc in next st. Flatten the opening and work 4 sc through both layers to close. Fasten off and cut off the yarn.

Legs (make 2):
Rnd 1: 7 sc in magic ring [7]
Rnd 2: (inc) repeat 7 times [14]
Rnd 3: (sc in next st, inc) repeat 7 times 21]
Rnd 4-5: sc in all 21 st [21]
Rnd 6: sc in next 3 st, (sc in next st, dec) repeat 5 times, sc in next 3 st [16]
Rnd 7: sc in next 3 st, (dec) × 5 times, sc in next 3 st 11]
Rnd 8: sc in next 4 st, dec, sc in next 5 st 10]
Rnd 9-13 (5 rounds): sc in all 10 st [10]
Firmly stuff the foot only. Leave the rest of the leg unstuffed to allow it to lay flat against the body. Flatten the opening and work 5 sc through both layers to close. Fasten off and cut off the yarn.

Tail:
Rnd 1: 5 sc in magic ring 15]
Rnd 2-8 (7 rounds): sc in all 5 st [5]
Rnd 9: inc, sc in next 4 st [6]
Rnd 10: inc, sc in next 5 st 7]
Rnd 11: inc, sc in next 6 st 8]
Don't stuff. Flatten the opening and work 4 sc through both layers to close. Fasten off and cut off the yarn.

Ears (make 2):
Crochet 4 ch. Continue working in turning rows, beginning in 2nd ch from the hook.
Row 1: inc, sc in next st, inc [5]
Row 2: 1 ch, inc, sc in next 3 st, inc 7]
Row 3-4: 1 ch, sc in next 7 st [7]
Row 5: 1 ch, dec, sc in next 3 st, dec [5)
Row 6: 1 ch, sc in next 5 st [5]
Row 7:1 ch, dec, sc in next st, dec [3]
Row 8: 1 ch, sc in next 3 st [3]

Make 1 ch. Crochet sc around 3 sides of the ear, working an inc in the corners marked by dots on the photo.
Fasten off leaving a tail for sewing.

Body:
Stuff as you go.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring [6]
Rnd 2: (inc) repeat 6 times (2)
Rnd 3: (sc in next st, inc) repeat 6 times 18)
Rnd 4: (sc in next 2 st, inc) repeat 6 times [24] Rnd 5: (sc in next 3 st, inc) repeat 6 times [30]
Attach the legs and tail in the next two rounds by crocheting through both the stitches of the limb and the body simultaneously.
Rnd 6: sc in next 5 st, sc in next 5 st through both the leg and the body, sc in next 10 st, sc in next 5 st through both another leg and the body, sc in next 3 st, sc in next 2 st through both the tail and the body [30] Rnd 7: sc in next 2 st through both the tail and the body, sc in next 28 st [30]
The stitch marker must remain centered along the back. If it starts to drift, work one or two additional single crochets to get back to the center.

Rnd 8: sc in next 6 st, (sc in next st, dec) repeat 6 times, sc in next 6 st [24) Rnd 9: sc in next 7 st, dec, sc in next 6 st, dec, sc in next 7 st 22] Rnd 10: sc in next 8 st, dec, sc in next 2 st, dec, sc in next 8 st [20] Rnd 11: sc in next 8 st, (dec) x 2 times, sc in next 8 st [18]
Rnd 12-13: sc in all 18 st [18]
Attach the arms in the next round. Ensure the arms stay centered on the sides as you attach them.
Rnd 14: sc in next 2 st, sc in next 4 st through both the arm and the body, sc in next 6 st, sc in next 4 st through both another arm and the body, sc in next 2 st [18]
Rnd 15: (sc in next st, dec) repeat 6 times [12
Rnd 16: sc in all 12 st [12]
Fasten off, leaving a tail for sewing.

Making the eye indents:
- ﻿﻿Mark two points at the base of the head (between Rnd 7 and Rnd 8), centered directly below the eyes. Leave 1-2 stitches between these pins.
- ﻿﻿Thread a needle with a matching yarn and "sculpt" the face by pulling the yarn from the eye to the marked point at the base. Repeat for the second eye.
- ﻿﻿Once the eyes are sunken in to create a cute puppy look, knot the yarn tightly and bury the ends inside the toy.

Shaping the muzzle:
- ﻿﻿Mark two anchor points at the bottom center of the muzzle between Rnd 3 and Rnd 4, leaving 1-2 stitches between them.
- ﻿﻿Form the mouth by following the path shown in the photo, pulling the yarn through the muzzle to the anchor points.
- ﻿﻿Pull the yarn firmly to create the desired indentation. Pull tight enough so the thread sinks into the indent, then secure it with a knot.
- ﻿﻿Repeat the sculpting stitches 2-3 times to ensure the mouth shape is well-defined and holds its form.

Lower jaw:
Crochet 3 ch. Continue working around the foundation chain, beginning in 2nd ch from the hook.
Rnd 1: sc in next ch, 3 sc in last ch, turn, sc in next ch 15]
Fasten off leaving a tail for sewing.

Assembly:
1. ﻿﻿﻿Position the lower jaw and secure it with pins to ensure it stays centered, then sew it to the head
2. ﻿﻿﻿Sew the head to the body. Position the muzzle at a slight downward tilt for a cuter expression. Add some fiberfill to the neck before finishing the seam.
3. ﻿﻿﻿Position the ears between Rnd 9 and Rnd 15 and secure them with pins. Once aligned, sew them to the head, ensuring there are 9-10 stitches of space between the ears.
4. ﻿﻿﻿Embroider the eye whites with white plush yarn.
5. ﻿﻿﻿Use black thread to embroider the eyelids and eyebrows. To add extra detail, you can stitch a small line beneath the nose.
